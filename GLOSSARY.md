# Glossary

**Cliente** — A person who rents vehicles and owns the requests, contracts, and credit contracts created in their name.

**Agente** — A user who works for a rental company or a bank and operates the system on behalf of that organization.

**Locadora** — A company that owns vehicles and rents them to clients.

**Banco** — A financial institution that offers credit contracts used to finance a rental or a vehicle.

**Automóvel** — A vehicle in the rental fleet, identified by registration, plate, brand, model, year, and daily rate.

**Pedido de Aluguel** — A client's request to rent a specific vehicle for a date range.

**Contrato de Aluguel** — The agreement that turns an approved rental request into an active rental, recording the vehicle, dates, total, ownership type, and status.

**Contrato de Crédito** — A financing agreement between a bank and a client, with an amount, installment count, interest rate, and approval status.

**Proprietário** — The party that owns a vehicle: a client, a rental company, or a bank.

**Parecer** — The agent's written assessment of a rental request, especially the justification for a rejection.

**Status do Pedido** — The stage of a rental request in its lifecycle, from submission through review, contracting, and completion or cancellation.
